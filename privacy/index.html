Privacy Notice

Polyfields, Inc. (trading as Trade Quest, Clipco and Green Grove Products) operates internal software that connects to Amazon services — including Amazon Advertising and Selling Partner APIs — using Login with Amazon. This notice describes how that software handles data.

What the software accesses

Advertising campaign settings and performance reports, listing content, inventory, orders and financial data for the Polyfields, Inc. seller and advertising accounts only. It is used solely by Polyfields, Inc. to manage its own business.

What is stored

Reports and account data are stored on Polyfields-owned computers for internal analysis. Authentication tokens are stored in the operating system keychain and are never shared. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than operating the Polyfields, Inc. business.

Personal information

The software does not collect personal information from shoppers beyond what Amazon provides to sellers for order fulfilment, and that information is retained only as long as Amazon's policies require.
